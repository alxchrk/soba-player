FFmpeg (ffmpeg, ffprobe) 8.1.3
------------------------------
License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later
         (see FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt and FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt)
Website: https://ffmpeg.org
Source:  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.3.tar.xz
         A copy of this exact source archive is attached to every
         Soba Player release on https://github.com/alxchrk/soba-player/releases

The ffmpeg and ffprobe executables in Contents/Resources/ffmpeg are built from
the unmodified FFmpeg 8.1.3 source by build/ffmpeg/build.sh in the Soba Player
repository. The build uses no GPL or non-free components and links no external
libraries other than those shipped with macOS. Configuration:

  --arch=arm64 --cc=clang
  --extra-cflags=-mmacosx-version-min=11.0
  --extra-ldflags=-mmacosx-version-min=11.0
  --extra-libs=-liconv
  --disable-autodetect --enable-videotoolbox --enable-audiotoolbox
  --enable-zlib --enable-bzlib --enable-iconv
  --enable-static --disable-shared
  --disable-ffplay --disable-doc --disable-debug

Soba Player runs these executables as separate processes. You may replace them
with your own build of FFmpeg by putting ffmpeg and ffprobe into
Contents/Resources/ffmpeg, or by setting the FFMPEG_PATH and FFPROBE_PATH
environment variables.
